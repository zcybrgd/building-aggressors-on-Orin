Baseline (default DVFS)

Make sure no locks: nvidia-smi -rgc and -rmc in admin shell.​

Start monitor: python dvfs_poc.py monitor

In another terminal: python dvfs_poc.py benchmark

Observe:

Average SM/MEM clocks

Temperature curve

Kernel times printed by DLL

Locked low clocks (DVFS reduced)

In admin PowerShell: nvidia-smi -lgc 600,600 and optionally -lmc to a lower mem clock.​

Repeat monitor + benchmark.

Expect: lower clocks, longer kernel time, lower temp/power.

Locked high clocks

E.g. nvidia-smi -lgc 1600,1600 if supported by your GPU.​

Repeat, compare exec time and reported clocks.

Thermal throttling exploration

Use a slightly heavier iters (e.g. 10000) and/or repeated runs to heat the GPU.

Watch temperature and SM clock: you should see the SM clock step down automatically once a thermal or power limit is hit (DVFS throttling).
